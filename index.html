The Challenge

Why MSMEs Need More Accessible Automation

Many Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises face significant barriers when trying to adopt industrial automation for their manufacturing processes.

High Upfront Costs

Industrial robots and automation systems often require substantial capital investment that can be prohibitive for smaller manufacturers.

Complex Programming

Traditional industrial robots require specialized programming knowledge and complex integration processes that smaller factories may not have access to.

Maintenance Expenses

Ongoing maintenance, repair costs, and the need for specialized technical support can add significantly to the total cost of ownership.

Limited Skilled Workers

Access to workers with robotics and automation expertise can be limited, particularly for MSMEs in smaller manufacturing hubs.

Repetitive Manual Tasks

Workers spend valuable time on repetitive manufacturing tasks that could potentially be automated, limiting productivity and growth.

Integration Time

Setting up and integrating automation into existing workflows often takes considerable time and can disrupt ongoing production.

Target Development Cost

MECHABOTS is targeting a development cost of ₹2–3 lakh for its modular cobot concept. This target will be refined through component selection, prototype testing, supplier quotations, and manufacturing cost analysis.

This is an initial development cost target — not a confirmed manufacturing cost or selling price.

Cost-Conscious Design Approach

  • Practical Component Selection — Choosing readily available, cost-effective components without compromising essential functionality.
  • Modular Design — Allowing individual modules to be replaced or upgraded without replacing the entire system.
  • Reusable End-Effectors — Interchangeable grippers and tools that can serve multiple tasks.
  • Easier Maintenance — Designing for straightforward maintenance to reduce long-term costs.
  • MSME-Focused Requirements — Targeting the specific needs and constraints of small manufacturers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about MECHABOTS and our modular cobot project.

MECHABOTS is a student startup project based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. We are developing a low-cost modular collaborative robot (cobot) concept designed for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

A collaborative robot, or cobot, is a robot designed to work alongside human operators in shared workspaces. Cobots typically feature safety provisions such as force limiting and collision detection to support safer human-robot interaction.

The robot aims to support pick-and-place operations, basic sorting, screw tightening with suitable attachments, and sensor-based object detection. Task feasibility depends on the completed design, payload capacity, and object characteristics.
